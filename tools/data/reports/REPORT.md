## Specs flagged as doubtful

Our own notes (`doubt:` in drinks_master.yaml):

- **Clover Club**: IBA lists only "a few drops" of egg white; we use about half a white (15 ml) for a proper foam.
- **Daiquiri**: IBA sweetens with 2 bar spoons of superfine sugar; we use 15 ml simple syrup, the common modern equivalent.
- **Monkey Gland**: IBA lists a tablespoon each of absinthe and grenadine; we use a bar spoon of absinthe and 10 ml grenadine (closer to the original Savoy-era spec). Check to taste.
- **Planter's Punch**: IBA uses 30 ml sugar-cane juice with no lengthener, which is very sweet and short; we suggest optional soda. Many bars add Angostura and grenadine.
- **Zombie**: IBA lists "Demerara rum" without saying overproof; the 1934 original used 151-proof. We assume overproof, so ABV is on the high side.
- **Tommy's Margarita**: IBA lists 30 ml agave nectar; the bar's own spec is often closer to 15 ml of agave syrup cut 1:1 with water. Taste and adjust.
- **Breakfast Martini**: Modern classic (London, 1990s); spec varies. Ours follows the common 50/15/15 plus a bar spoon of marmalade.
- **Lemon, Lime and Bitters**: Listed as a mocktail, but the bitters add a trace of alcohol (well under 1% in the glass).
