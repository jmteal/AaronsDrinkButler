## Spoken recipes

- The spoken recipes in the device pack (`speech/*.opk`, built by `tools/data/build.py pack`) are read from our own recipe text and generated on the Mac with **Parler-TTS Mini v1** (`parler-tts/parler-tts-mini-v1`, model and code by Hugging Face, **Apache License 2.0**): https://huggingface.co/parler-tts/parler-tts-mini-v1 and https://github.com/huggingface/parler-tts . The generated audio is covered by the repository's licenses (CC BY 4.0, like the recipe text it reads).
- The optional `--verify` check transcribes the clips locally with OpenAI Whisper (`small`, MIT) via faster-whisper (MIT); nothing from it is bundled.
- The device decodes the clips with Espressif's `esp_audio_codec` (Opus).
